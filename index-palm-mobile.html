🚀 Key Innovations vs Desktop Version
1. Palm Gesture Trigger
Explicit summon signal replacing automatic triggers. Open palm + 1-second hold prevents accidental activations.
2. Sequential Processing
Process one item at a time with visual progress tracking. Completed items show checkmarks and turn gray.
3. Mobile Performance
Detection every 300ms (not per frame), max 5 objects, 30 particles. Optimized for battery and thermal management.
4. Touch Fallback
If gesture recognition fails or is difficult, users can always tap buttons directly. Best of both worlds.
5. Landscape Lock
Forces landscape mode with visual warning in portrait. Ensures optimal camera FOV and button layout.
6. Center Focus
Move phone to align item with center circle (200px radius). Palm gesture triggers decision panel for focused item.
