# Life Choices - Interactive Game

An interactive life choice game with multiple endings and minigames.

## Play Online

🎮 **[Click here to play the game!](https://yzhu24-ship-it.github.io/siren/)**

## Features

- 🎯 11 different life endings
- 🎮 4 interactive minigames:
  - Sudoku Challenge
  - Geography Quiz
  - Code Debugging
  - Hurdles Running
- 🚪 Choice-based gameplay
- 🎨 Beautiful wooden doors and blackboard design
- 🌍 Full English version

## How to Play

1. Click the doors to make your life choices
2. Complete minigames to progress
3. Discover different endings based on your decisions

## Local Play

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser.
